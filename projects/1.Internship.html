Internship

1. Internship

During my third year at SintLucas, I undertook a unique and highly unconventional internship with the Royal Netherlands Army. Stepping completely outside the traditional comfort zone of a standard game studio or IT agency, I embedded with a multi-disciplinary team from different schools to develop a specialized serious game. Working in this high-stakes, unfamiliar environment required rapid adaptation, emotional maturity, and the ability to collaborate effectively without a pre-existing professional network.

As the Game Designer, I served as the strategic anchor for the team. I pioneered the design process by authoring and maintaining a comprehensive Game Design Document (GDD). The GDD functioned as the project’s blueprint, systematically cataloging our core mechanics, iterative design loops, technical constraints, and risk-mitigation strategies.

This experience marked a pivotal evolution in my professional methodology. It fundamentally shifted my design philosophy from an ad-hoc, execution-first approach to a highly structured, analytical, and concept-driven framework rooted in discipline and strategic intent.

For a more in-depth look, please take a look at 'Internship Report' here

01 / From Reactive to Intentional Design (Concept Thinking)

This role disciplined me to abandon "building before thinking." I established a rigorous conceptual framework where every mechanic was analytically mapped to user objectives and project constraints before entering the production phase.

02 / Comprehensive GDD Ownership & System Alignment

I took full ownership of the project's documentation. The resulting GDD did not just list features; it acted as a living analytical archive that synchronized our multi-disciplinary team, tracked development pivots, and justified design choices against technical limitations.
