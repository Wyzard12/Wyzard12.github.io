2. Internship

If my first internship was about stepping out of my comfort zone, my final graduation internship was a rigorous stress test of my accumulated skills, experience, and professional resilience. With the clear ambition of securing a spot at Breda University of Applied Sciences (BUas)—one of the most prestigious game design institutions in the country—I knew I needed to proactively validate my capabilities under the highest pressure. There was no better arena to test my grit and design execution than returning to the Royal Netherlands Army.

I chose to return to complete the loop, fulfilling a personal commitment to tackle the complex challenges I had left unfinished in my first tenure. This decision proved to be one of the most defining turning points in my early career.

For over four months, I willingly immersed myself in a high-intensity workspace, embracing every demanding task assigned by my supervisor. Working with completely new teams and ambiguous constraints allowed me to objectively audit my technical limits, master stress management, and embrace constructive friction. This "trial by fire" culminated in a highly successful internship, providing me with an invaluable, rock-solid foundation in structured systems thinking and cross-disciplinary leadership. I left not only meeting critical milestones but with a definitive blueprint of my strengths and specific areas for future growth.

To have a deeper insight, please take a look at '2. Internship Report.'

01 / Proactive Resilience & Stress Management

Intentionally placed myself in a high-pressure, strict operational ecosystem to test my adaptability. I mastered the ability to maintain design clarity, meet hard deadlines, and make sound systemic choices under corporate and institutional stress.

02 / Objective Self-Audit & Analytical Mindset

Approached the entire 4-month lifecycle as a professional post-mortem. By intentionally seeking out my technical weaknesses and limitations, I converted design friction points into actionable areas for future skill optimization.

03 / Strategic Ambition & Growth Orientation

Aligned the high standards of a military-grade serious game with the strict, top-tier expectations required for my transition to higher education at BUas, proving my readiness for enterprise-level design challenges.
